Two modes

Pick a mode when the app opens. Both stay invisible to everyone else.

Exam mode is for anything on your screen. Interview mode is for anything you hear.

How it works

Three keystrokes from question to answer.

Step 1

Capture

Press Ctrl+Shift+S for the whole screen or Ctrl+Shift+Z to drag out a region. In Interview mode, Ctrl+Shift+V starts listening instead.

Step 2

Ask

Press Ctrl+Shift+P. Your question goes to the providers you turned on in Settings, with your notes or documents attached if you want them.

Step 3

Read, then hide

Answers stream in with formatted code and math. Ctrl+Shift+C copies the latest one. Ctrl+Shift+A hides the window until you need it again.

What's inside

Everything you need for the question in front of you.

No account, no subscription. Add the keys you already have and it works.

01

Five providers, side by side

Send one question to OpenAI, Gemini, Claude, Z.AI and a local LM Studio model at once. Each answer streams into its own column, so you can compare before you trust.Or turn on just one. A Test button checks each key and model name.

02

Full and region capture

Grab the whole screen or a precise crop, accurate on high-DPI displays. Queue up to five screenshots to give the AI more context.

03

Text extraction on your machine

Turn screenshots into text before they are sent. Faster, cheaper, and it lets text-only local models read your screen.Dozens of languages. The first run downloads a small language file, then works offline.

04

Live listening

Records the audio coming out of your call, not just your microphone. Transcribes with Whisper, Gemini or OpenAI realtime, then answers in the language the question was asked in.

05

Your documents as context

Import PDF, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, CSV, JSON or plain text. The AI pulls out the key points on import, and answers are grounded in your file first.

06

Notes with math

Keep working notes that render Markdown, highlighted code and LaTeX as you type. Attach a note to your questions, or show it next to the answers.

07

Answer styles and follow-ups

Choose code with explanation, a detailed walkthrough, or just the letter for multiple choice. Continue any answer as a conversation. Save the system prompts you reuse.

08

History and cost

Every question and answer is saved on your machine. Search it, star it, tag it, export it as Markdown or JSON. Each answer shows its tokens and estimated cost, with a running total per provider.

Privacy

Only you can see it.

The window is excluded from screen capture at the operating-system level. Recordings, screen shares and screenshots taken by other apps show what is behind it, not the app itself.
