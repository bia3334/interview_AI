Hands stay off the keyboard

Hear the question, answer it instantly

Press Ctrl+Shift+V and Screen Genius records the system audio coming out of your call — the interviewer's actual voice — together with a screenshot. It transcribes with Whisper or Gemini and sends both to your models automatically. No typing, no tell-tale tab switching.

  • ✓ Captures system audio, not just your mic
  • ✓ Pairs the transcript with a full or region screenshot
  • ✓ Auto-sends to every selected provider

Behind a hotkey

From screen to answer in seconds

No window switching, no uploads, no fuss. Every step is a global shortcut.

1

Capture

Full shot with Ctrl+Shift+S, crop a region with Ctrl+Shift+Z, or pull text out with OCR via Ctrl+Shift+X.

2

Ask

Type, paste the clipboard with Ctrl+Shift+Q, or speak with Ctrl+Shift+V. Then analyze with Ctrl+Shift+P.

3

Read & vanish

Answers stream into the window and overlay. Copy with Ctrl+Shift+C, then hide everything instantly with Ctrl+Shift+A.

Built to stay unseen

Invisible where it counts — and yours alone

Screen Genius turns on OS-level content protection, so the window is excluded from screen captures, recordings and shared sessions. Flip click-through mode and your clicks pass straight through to whatever's behind it. Nothing leaves your machine except the request you send to your own AI provider.

  • ✓ Hidden from screen-recording & screen-share software
  • ✓ Click-through mode lets the mouse pass right through
  • ✓ Instant show/hide and a panic key that wipes everything and quits
  • ✓ Your keys, captures and history stay local — no account, no telemetry
