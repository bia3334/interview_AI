Three keystrokes

From screen to answer in seconds

No window switching, no uploads, no fuss. It all happens behind a hotkey.

1

Capture

Hit Ctrl+Shift+S for a full shot or Ctrl+Shift+Z to crop a region. OCR text with Ctrl+Shift+X.

2

Ask

Type, paste your clipboard with Ctrl+Shift+Q, or speak. Then send it all to AI with Ctrl+Shift+P.

3

Read & vanish

The answer streams into the overlay. Copy it with Ctrl+Shift+C and hide everything with Ctrl+Shift+A.

Built to stay unseen

Invisible where it counts

Screen Genius uses OS-level content protection, so it's excluded from screen captures, recordings, and shared sessions. Toggle click-through mode and it stops intercepting your mouse entirely — your work shows through, the app doesn't.
